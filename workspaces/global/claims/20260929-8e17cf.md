(1) scripts/screening/screen.py: backtest() only ever entered a position - pos set at :49, never reset, sole exit at the final bar - so n_trades was ALWAYS 1 and results.json recorded trades:1 in every cell, voiding the '0/15 concepts' row of ALPHA_SEARCH_TERMINAL_REPORT.md:27. Fixed by treating side_fn(i) as the DESIRED position (BUY long / SELL short / None flat), closing and reopening at the SAME next-bar open so a flip earns no free intrabar move; every concept in make_concepts() is already target-position shaped so no signal-fn change was needed. Verified on synthetic bars: flipping signal 1 -> 20 trades, always-long -> 1, never-signals -> 0. BARS_PER_YEAR=2190 deliberately NOT changed (should be 1610 for Dukascopy 4h) because it also moves the 200 pips/yr kill threshold - that is a gate re-registration, not a bug fix. (2) src/titan/execution/engine.py: fees=Money('0') was hardcoded at :736, so paper fills were free and any paper session would overstate PnL by the full cost. Added PaperConfig.commission_bps/spread_bps, _fill_fee() from fill notional, cost_model_configured property, and BrokerFill now reports the real fee. UNRESOLVED LIMIT: PortfolioEngine.apply_fill in core/src/portfolio.rs has signature (instrument_id, side, quantity, price) with no way to debit cash, and cargo/rustc are not installed so the extension cannot be rebuilt - the fee is reported but not yet booked against portfolio cash. Rust deliberately untouched because it cannot be compiled or tested here. (3) replicated tri-state in crypto_screen.py, equities_factor_screen_006.py, equities_event_screen_008.py: the default was bool=False so the replication gate could never pass and verdict was a CONSTANT rather than a measurement; CRYPTO-001 passed 4/5 gates and EQ-007 passed 5/9 (incl OOS Sharpe 1.249) yet both were filed as failures.
