**Claim:** METHOD LESSON (applies beyond CRYPTO-006): a trade-reversal top-of-book proxy cannot be used to RANK edge across liquidity tiers, because its upward bias is proportional to the very quantity being measured. In CRYPTO-006, corr(measured_half_spread, net_edge)=0.967 — near-perfect monotonicity between outcome and proxy bias is the signature of measuring the artifact, not the mechanism. On a thin book a reversal gap is mostly price impact + tick discretisation, not reclaimable quoted spread. Consequences: (1) G3-style fill-COUNT gates are vacuous under this proxy since it ignores cancel latency and queue position and over-books fills — never cite a passed capacity gate as feasibility evidence; (2) paid historical L2 does NOT rescue such a probe: L2 sharpens the gross-spread term the proxy already overstates, so it mostly buys confirmation of rejection, and cannot repair a robustness gate whose quantity has sign-matched bias; (3) reproduction check passed - re-measuring BTCUSDT gave -2.07bps vs CRYPTO-005 sealed -2.04bps, so the code is sound for RELATIVE tier comparison even though the absolute level is inflated. Also verified: Binance Vision publishes ONLY aggTrades/trades/klines - there is NO historical bookTicker or depth on Vision (checked S3 listing data/spot/monthly/).
