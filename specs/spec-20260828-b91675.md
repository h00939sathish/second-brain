# Spec: Cross-Market Alpha Research Admission and Scope

## Overview
Purpose: establish why TITAN may investigate cross-market alpha after terminal FX OHLCV, equities-factor, and crypto discovery results, while preventing infra-before-alpha.

Invariants:
- The terminal findings remain absorbing for their registered hypothesis IDs and signal families. Cross-market work cannot repackage, invert, retune, relax costs for, or extend those failures.
- 'Cross-market alpha' means a measurable, causal, cross-instrument transfer or dislocation that is not a directional liquid-OHLCV pattern. It is not a portfolio diversification claim, correlation mining exercise, or execution program.
- Research authority is read-only. No credentials, adapters, TradeIntent, portfolio/risk changes, paper accounts, order paths, PromotionCertificate changes, or live-capital authority.
- Data is acquired before hypothesis evaluation and must include provider/licence, PIT availability time, UTC timestamps, symbol/contract mapping, corrections, survivorship treatment, checksums, and retention policy. Absence of an admissible data contract is a terminal pre-experiment rejection, not an implementation request.
- An admissible hypothesis has a distinct ID, causal mechanism, counter-mechanism, exact eligible instruments, decision timestamp, lead/lag convention, IS/OOS seal, random and economically relevant controls, capacity bound, immutable cost model, stress cases, and quantitative pass/fail gates declared before OOS read.
- Simulation uses actual executable instruments and quote-sided/top-of-book fills when available. It separately attributes gross mechanism return, spread, fees, impact, financing/borrow, FX conversion, data latency, legging/hedge failures, and capacity. Bar-close constant-bps work is exploratory only and never qualifying.
- A failure is permanently preserved and classified as mechanism_failure or execution_constrained_rejection, with evidence and confidence; a new mechanism requires a new hypothesis ID.
- A positive research result is only a candidate for a later shadow-only proposal. It does not authorize paper or live trading.

Admission scope:
1) Start with a data-availability and causal-mechanism screen, not platform construction. Candidates must use information absent from the terminal liquid-OHLCV search: timestamped macro/fundamental surprise data, synchronized cross-venue/cash-futures/ETF quote data, or validated participant/flow/order-book data.
2) Prioritize a single hypothesis only after the screen: CM-001 may test scheduled, timestamped macro-surprise transmission across liquid US ETFs, but only if licensed consensus, release timestamps, revision/vintage fields, and synchronized executable prices meet the data contract. This is event-driven relative response, not raw directional forecast.
3) Cash-futures/ETF dislocation and market-structure/flow hypotheses are deferred unless their own data quality, licenses, executable-price, capacity, and venue-risk reviews pass. Crypto remains research-only under ADR-029 and CRYPTO-001..003 stay closed; CRYPTO-004 remains its independently sealed mechanism, not a substitute for this program.

Acceptance evidence for the planning artifact: proposed ADR and charter name owners/boundary/interfaces/failure modes; contain a hypothesis exclusion registry; define pre-data and post-data gates, rollback and monitoring; cite terminal records; make no alpha or readiness claim; have no execution imports or code changes. Council acceptance is required before any implementation task.
